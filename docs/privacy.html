Privacy Policy

YouTube Mix Playlist Generator

Overview

YouTube Mix Playlist Generator is designed with privacy in mind. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. All processing happens locally inside your browser.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

  • No name
  • No email address
  • No password
  • No payment information
  • No browsing history
  • No location data
  • No analytics or tracking

Local Storage

The extension stores the following information only on your own device using Chrome Storage API.

  • Theme preference
  • Language preference
  • Auto Copy preference
  • Show Button preference
  • Recently generated playlist history

This information never leaves your browser.

Network Requests

The extension communicates only with YouTube to generate playlist URLs. No user information is sent to any external server controlled by the developer.

Permissions

  • tabs — Detects the active YouTube Mix page.
  • storage — Saves your extension preferences and recent playlist history locally.
  • https://www.youtube.com/* — Required to access YouTube Mix pages and generate playlists.

Third-Party Services

This extension does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Analytics platforms
  • Tracking scripts
  • Remote databases
  • User profiling services

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect new features or improvements. Any changes will be published on this page.
